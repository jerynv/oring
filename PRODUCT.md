# Product

## Platform

ios

## Stack

Native SwiftUI, CoreBluetooth, and a Rust UniFFI core based on the MIT-licensed open_health/open_oura projects. The Xcode project is generated by XcodeGen.

## Users

Owners of a compatible Oura ring who want a free, private iPhone app that reads the ring directly over Bluetooth. The user explicitly chose direct Bluetooth and asked for REM sleep patterns and detailed graphs.

## Product Purpose

Pair with or authenticate to a ring, sync its on-device event history, and present sleep stages and overnight signals in a readable daily report. Success requires a real device sync, honest provenance, and a clearly labeled sample report for exploration without hardware.

## Positioning

An independent, local-first ring almanac with no app subscription, account, ads, or server.

## Operating Context

A ring already paired by another app needs its existing 32-character pairing key. A factory-reset ring can be paired in Oring. The user places the ring on its charger, frees its Bluetooth connection from other phones, and syncs. Factory reset erases unsynced ring data. The simulator can exercise the UI and sample report, but cannot validate real Bluetooth behavior.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app reads ring data directly over CoreBluetooth; Apple Health is not used.
- The reverse-engineered protocol is not an official Oura integration, so compatibility may change with firmware.
- The ring's own sleep-stage stream supports deep, light, REM, and awake periods. Detailed sleep reports show an interactive hypnogram and aligned heart rate, HRV, oxygen, skin temperature, and motion lanes when those signals exist.
- No proprietary Oura scores or bundled decrypted models. Any independent score must be labeled as such.
- Pairing keys remain in iOS Keychain; synced events live in the app's local SQLite store. No server or tracking.
- App Store distribution requires a signed device build, a physical-ring test, a public privacy-policy URL, and Apple review.

## Evidence on Hand

- MIT-licensed open_health at commit `5569be1` and pinned open_oura dependency at `99e0f4c4`; the latter documents direct BLE pairing, event sync, and ring sleep-phase data.
- Simulator UI and sample report tests passed. Fresh pairing, live battery, and event sync were exercised on an iPhone 16 Pro Max with a physical ring. An overnight sleep-stage report still needs a captured night.

## Product Principles

- Show the source and say when data is sample or unavailable.
- Make pairing consequences explicit before any destructive step.
- Keep personal ring data on-device.
- Use Apple system typography and a quiet dark space setting. Guide pairing one step at a time, with technical controls available on request.
