# Oring Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Oring connects to a compatible ring over Bluetooth when you choose to pair or sync. It reads the device's event history, including sleep stages and available heart, movement, temperature, and oxygen signals. Oring uses these records to draw your daily and overnight reports on your iPhone.

Ring events and derived summaries are stored locally in the app's SQLite database. Your ring pairing key is stored in iOS Keychain. Diagnostic records are kept on-device and can be exported only when you choose an export action. The sample night is synthetic and is always labeled. Oring does not create an account, use analytics or tracking SDKs, show ads, or send ring data to a server. It does not access Apple Health.

You can remove Oring's local app data by deleting the app. Pairing keys saved in Keychain may persist according to iOS Keychain behavior; the app offers controls for local data and ring management. Exported files are controlled by the destination you choose.

Oring is an independent app and is not affiliated with Oura Health. For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on Oring's App Store page.
